Description

Bunny Grid Game is a 2D adventure puzzle game where players guide one or two bunnies through grid-based levels. The player must collect keys, avoid obstacles, and find the correct path to unlock doors and complete each stage.

Game Demo Video

Screenshots

Movement Controls

Players use the W, A, S, and D keys to move the bunny across the grid. Enjoy solving puzzles with simple and intuitive gameplay.

Keys, Doors, and Evil Bunny

Collect keys to unlock doors, avoid different obstacles, escape from the evil bunny chasing you, and complete each stage!

3D Cube Puzzles

Some levels transform the grid into a 3D cube. Guide the bunny across connected sides, collect the key, avoid obstacles, and reach the door.
